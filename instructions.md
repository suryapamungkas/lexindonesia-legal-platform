# SYSTEM PROMPT FOR AI AGENT: BUILDING LEGAL PLATFORM WEBSITE

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
Develop a modern, highly scalable, and professional Legal Platform website modeled after Hukumonline's product catalog ecosystem. The platform serves legal professionals, corporations, academic institutions, and the general public by providing access to legal data, intelligence tools, compliance systems, and educational courses.

---

## 2. DESIGN & BRANDING SPECIFICATIONS
* **Color Palette:**
  * Primary: Deep Navy Blue (`#002147` / `#0B192C`)
  * Secondary / Accent: Bright Yellow / Gold (`#FFB800` / `#FFC107`)
  * Backgrounds: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) and Light Off-White (`#F8F9FA`)
  * Text Primary: Dark Charcoal (`#1A1A1A`)
  * Text Muted: Slate Gray (`#6C757D`)
* **Typography:**
  * Primary Font: Sans-Serif (e.g., Inter, Open Sans, or Roboto)
  * Headings: Bold, clean, and high-contrast
* **UI/UX Design Patterns:**
  * Modern Card-Based Grid layout for products and services.
  * Clear visual hierarchy with distinct section dividers.
  * Highly accessible forms and search inputs with dark-mode header option.
  * Responsive execution across Mobile, Tablet, and Desktop viewports.

---

## 3. PAGE STRUCTURE & LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE

### A. Header Navigation Bar
1. **Top Utility Bar:**
   * Global Product Catalog Link
   * Subscription Call-to-Action (CTA) button ("Berlangganan Pro")
   * Language Switcher Dropdown (e.g., ID / EN)
2. **Main Navigation Row:**
   * Brand Logo
   * Global Search Bar with icon (`Cari peraturan, putusan, artikel...`)
   * Dropdown Categories:
     * `Pro`
     * `Solusi`
     * `Info Hukum`
     * `Event & Awards`
   * User Authentication: `Daftar` (Register) & `Masuk` (Login) buttons

### B. Hero Section
* **Background:** High-contrast dark imagery with legal motif overlays.
* **Heading:** Large, authoritative value proposition (e.g., "Indonesia’s One-Stop Legal Platform").
* **Sub-heading:** Summary statement describing integrated legal products and solutions.
* **Primary CTA:** "Hubungi Kami" (Contact Us) button.

### C. Product & Service Grid Sections

#### 1. Category: PRO (Premium Legal Intelligence & Data)
* **Pusat Data:** Systematized database of laws, regulations, and court decisions.
* **Analisa Hukum:** Bilingual comprehensive legal analysis on modern issues.
* **Premium Stories:** Practical reference articles for legal practitioners.
* **Legal Intelligence Updates:** Exclusives on draft regulations and government policies.
* **My Workspace:** Virtual workspace features for legal research management.

#### 2. Category: SOLUSI (Enterprise & Tech Solutions)
* **University Solutions:** Integrated knowledge hub for academic networks.
* **Regulatory Compliance System (RCS):** AI-powered compliance tracking platform.
* **Document Management System:** Enterprise legal document repository.
* **Perizinan Usaha:** Business incorporation and licensing services.
* **Konsultasi & Pembuatan Dokumen:** On-demand legal advice and document generation.
* **Hukumonline 360:** All-in-one suite access.

#### 3. Category: INFO HUKUM (Research & Media)
* **Klinik:** Free community Q&A on legal issues.
* **Hukumonline Stream:** Video streaming content covering legal topics.
* **Jurnal & Berita:** Peer-reviewed law journals and daily legal news updates.
* **Datapribadi.id:** Specialized tracking on Personal Data Protection regulations.

#### 4. Category: EVENTS & AWARDS (Education & Recognition)
* **Events & Training / Online Course:** Webinars, certifications, and online training platforms.
* **PKPA:** Special Advocates Education Program with legal associations.
* **Awards & Publikasi Online:** Legal industry rankings, awards, and online content publication.

### D. Footer Architecture
* **Contact & Office Info:** Address, phone, fax, and official email links.
* **Social Media Icons:** Links to LinkedIn, Instagram, X (Twitter), Facebook, TikTok, YouTube.
* **Footer Navigation Column:** Categorized text links mirroring main categories.
* **Certifications & Compliance Badges:** ISO certifications (27001, 9001), Kominfo registration, copyright notice.

---

## 4. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS FOR DEVELOPERS

1. **Global Search Engine Integration:**
   * Implement indexed search with filters (By year, regulation type, practice area, or content type).
2. **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):**
   * Free User Tier: Access to public news, basic Klinik Q&A, public listings.
   * Pro / Subscription Tier: Unlocked access to full legal analysis, regulatory databases, and workspace tools.
3. **Interactive Components:**
   * Modal dialogs for cookie preferences and bookmark management.
   * Floating notification banner for promotional announcements.
   * Category hover/dropdown menus with smooth animation transitions.
